# Related work and project positioning

Checked 2026-10-22. These sources establish context, not originality of this
implementation or endorsement of its findings.

- [Vanguard: Rebalancing your portfolio](https://investor.vanguard.com/investor-resources-education/portfolio-management/rebalancing-your-portfolio).
  Its cash-flow section describes directing portfolio cash flows toward
  underweight assets. Cash-flow rebalancing is established practice. This project
  studies a particular tracking objective, contribution grid, and execution lag.
- [Investor.gov: Asset allocation, diversification, and rebalancing](https://www.investor.gov/additional-resources/general-resources/publications-research/info-sheets/beginners-guide-asset).
  The SEC education guide describes changing ongoing contributions to restore
  an allocation and considering the consequences of transactions.
- [Cai, Judd, and Xu: Numerical Solution of Dynamic Portfolio Optimization with
  Transaction Costs, NBER Working Paper 18709](https://www.nber.org/papers/w18709).
  Their dynamic portfolio work concerns transaction costs and no-trade regions.
  A static minimum-sale band calculation here is a different, narrower objective;
  it does not reproduce or solve their dynamic utility optimization.

No literature novelty claim is made. A fuller review and verified second-source
data are required before treating this as publication-quality financial research.
